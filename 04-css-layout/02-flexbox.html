Chrome Dino Game

The Chrome Dino Game was released in 2014. It is a simple game that appears when Google Chrome cannot connect to the internet.

Players control a dinosaur and avoid obstacles by jumping over them.

Untitled Goose Game

Untitled Goose Game was released in 2019 by House House. Players control a goose and complete tasks in a village.

The game involves solving puzzles and causing mischief around the village.

Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird was released in 2013. Players control a bird and navigate through obstacles without hitting them.

The game became popular because of its simple controls and difficulty.
